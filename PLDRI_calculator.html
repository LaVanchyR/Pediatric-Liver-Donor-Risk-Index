Interpretation notes

Research/clinical support tool: This calculator reproduces the manuscript's PLDRI point system and Supplementary Table 4 survival lookup. It does not independently predict outcomes or incorporate recipient-specific risk.

Supplementary Table 4 contains observed PLDRI scores 2–10. The app intentionally does not extrapolate survival probabilities for theoretical scores 1 or 11–14.

Living-donor 5-year graft survival reported in the supplement: 88.9%. The manuscript reports HR 1.19 (95% CI 1.10–1.28) for graft loss per one-point increase in PLDRI.

Source: supplied PLDRI manuscript and supplementary material. Survival values are directly transcribed from Supplementary Table 4.
